Chapter 12 Part I: Relevant Costs for Decisions

    Is it relevant?

    A cost or revenue is relevant to a decision only if it passes both tests:

    1. Future: it will happen in the future.
    2. Differs: it is different between the choices.
    • Not relevant Sunk costs fail test 1.
    • Not relevant Costs that are the same either way fail test 2.
    • Relevant Opportunity costs pass both tests.

    Special order: is there room?

    Count only the amounts that change if Surf Gear accepts the order.

    • If the order fits in spare capacity, there is no opportunity cost.
    • If it doesn't fit, the contribution margin of the regular sales you give up is an opportunity cost of the order.

    Special order: the minimum price

    The minimum price must cover:

    • every variable cost the order adds, and
    • the contribution margin of any regular sales the order pushes out (the opportunity cost).

    Minimum price = (costs the order adds + opportunity cost) ÷ order units

    0 (order fits in spare capacity)

    Make or buy?

    Compare only the costs that differ between making and buying.

    • Not relevant Fixed costs you keep paying either way.
    • Relevant Fixed costs that go away if you buy.
    • Relevant Income the freed capacity could earn: an opportunity cost of making.
